```rust
fn main() {
    let total: i32 = (1..=20).filter(|&x| x % 2 == 0).sum();
    println!("Total of even numbers between 1 and 20 inclusive: {}", total);
}
```

The issue in the original code is that it does not include a semicolon at the end of the statement `println!("Total of even numbers between 1 and 20 inclusive: {}", total);`. Without a semicolon, the compiler treats it as an expression and evaluates it to a value, which is `total`, instead of printing it. The correct way to print the value is by adding a semicolon at the end.
