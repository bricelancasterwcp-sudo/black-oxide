    let prod = (1..=6).map(|x| x as Int).product();

Explanation of the corrections:
- The first `let` declaration was missing a semicolon at the end.
- The second `let` declaration also had a misplaced semicolon.
- The correct syntax for creating a vector from 1 to 6 is `(1..=6).collect::<Vec<Int>>()`.
- The multiplication operator (`*`) should be applied directly between two values, not within parentheses.
