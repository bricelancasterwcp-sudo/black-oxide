```rust
fn main() {
    let mut sum = 0;
    for i in 1..=5 {
        sum += i.pow(3);
    }
    println!("The sum of cubes from 1 to 5 is: {}", sum);
}
```

This Rust program calculates the sum of cubes of each whole number from 1 to 5 by initializing a variable `sum` to zero, then iterating through the range from 1 to 5 (inclusive), and adding the cube of each integer to `sum`. Finally, it prints the total sum.
