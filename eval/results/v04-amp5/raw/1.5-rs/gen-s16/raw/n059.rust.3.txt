```rust
fn main() {
    let phrase = "when in doubt go home";
    let word_count = phrase.split_whitespace().count();
    let char_count = phrase.chars().count();

    println!("Words: {}", word_count);
    println!("Characters including spaces: {}", char_count);
}
```

This program will correctly print:
Words: 5
Characters including spaces: 21
