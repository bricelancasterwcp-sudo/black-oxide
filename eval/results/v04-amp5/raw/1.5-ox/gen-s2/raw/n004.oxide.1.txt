```rust
fn main() {
    let mut a = 1;
    let mut b = 1;

    for _ in 0..9 {
        let temp = a + b;
        a = b;
        b = temp;
    }

    println!("{}", b);
}
```

Explanation:
- `let mut a = 1;` and `let mut b = 1;` initialize the sequence with the first two terms.
- The loop runs 9 times, computing each term as the sum of the previous two (`temp` holds `a + b`, then `a` takes on `b`, and `b` becomes `temp`).
- After the loop, `b` contains the tenth term of the sequence, which is printed.
