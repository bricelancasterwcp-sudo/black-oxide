```rust
fn main() {
    let mut a = 1;
    let mut b = 1;

    for _ in 2..=9 {
        let temp = a + b;
        a = b;
        b = temp;
    }

    println!("{}", b);
}
```

Explanation of the code:
- We start by initializing `a` and `b` to 1, as per the sequence's definition.
- We then enter a loop that runs from 2 to 9 (inclusive), incrementing a counter each time through the loop.
- Inside the loop, we calculate the next term by adding the two previous terms (`temp = a + b`).
- We then update `a` and `b` to be the last two terms calculated in the loop (`a = b`, `b = temp`).
- After the loop ends, `b` will hold the tenth term of the sequence (since we incremented the counter by 9 before breaking out of the loop).
