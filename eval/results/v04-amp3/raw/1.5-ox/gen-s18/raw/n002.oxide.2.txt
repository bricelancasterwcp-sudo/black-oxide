```rust
fn main() {
    let result = (1..=6).map(|x| x as Int).product();
    println(result);
}
```

Explanation of the corrections:
- The error `unexpected character '|'` was due to a misplaced pipe symbol in the `map` function call. It should be part of the closure, not after the iterator.
- The error `expected field name, found DOT` was because there is no dot separating the method call from the argument in `product::<Int>()`. The correct format is `result.product::<Int>()`.
- The error `unexpected character ';'` was due to a misplaced semicolon at the end of the `map` function call. The `println(result)` should be on a separate line.
- The errors were resolved by removing the pipe symbol, adding a dot between the method call and argument, and moving the `println` statement to its own line.
