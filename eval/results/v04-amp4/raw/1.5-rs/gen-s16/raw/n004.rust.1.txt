```rust
fn main() {
    let mut a = 0;
    let mut b = 1;

    for _ in 2..=10 {
        let next_term = a + b;
        a = b;
        b = next_term;
    }

    println!("{}", a);
}
```

In this program, we first initialize the variables `a` and `b` to 1. Then, using a loop that runs from 2 to 10 (inclusive), we calculate the sum of the two previous terms (`a` and `b`) and assign it to `next_term`. After that, we update `a` and `b` to be equal to each other and `next_term`, respectively. Finally, we print the tenth term of the sequence, which is stored in `a`.
