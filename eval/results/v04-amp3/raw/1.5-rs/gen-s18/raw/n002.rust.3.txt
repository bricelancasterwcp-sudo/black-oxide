The issue in the original program was that it was trying to multiply a range of numbers starting from 1 to 6, but it wasn't handling the multiplication correctly. The `product()` method on a `Range` will only return the product of the range's elements, not the cumulative product.

- We call `.product()` on this range to compute the product of all the numbers in it.
- The result is stored in the `result` variable.
