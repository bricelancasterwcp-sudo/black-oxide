```rust
fn main() {
    let mut largest_power = 1;
    while largest_power * 2 <= 100 {
        largest_power *= 2;
    }
    println!("The biggest power of two not exceeding 100 is: {}", largest_power);
}
```

Explanation:
- We initialize `largest_power` to 1.
- We enter a loop that continues as long as `largest_power * 2` is less than or equal to 100.
- Inside the loop, we multiply `largest_power` by 2.
- After the loop finishes, `largest_power` will be the biggest power of two that does not exceed 100.
- We then print this value.
