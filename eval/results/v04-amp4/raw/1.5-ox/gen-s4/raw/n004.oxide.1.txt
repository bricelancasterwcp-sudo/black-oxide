```rust
fn main() {
    let mut a = 1;
    let mut b = 1;
    for _ in 2..10 {
        let c = a + b;
        a = b;
        b = c;
    }
    print(a); // 55
}
```

**Explanation**:
- We initialize `a` and `b` to 1, as the first two terms of the sequence are both 1.
- In each iteration of the loop (from the third term to the tenth), we calculate the next term (`c`) as the sum of `a` and `b`.
- We then update `a` to be the previous value of `b`, and `b` to be the new calculated `c`.
- After the loop completes, `a` will hold the tenth term of the sequence (which is 55).
