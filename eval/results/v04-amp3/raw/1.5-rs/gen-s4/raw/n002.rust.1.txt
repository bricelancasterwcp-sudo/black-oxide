```rust
fn main() {
    let mut product = 1;
    for i in 1..7 {
        product *= i;
    }
    println!("The product of numbers from 1 to 6 is: {}", product);
}
```

Explanation:
- We initialize a variable `product` to 1 because multiplying any number by 1 does not change the number.
- We use a `for` loop to iterate through all whole numbers from 1 to 6 (inclusive).
- Inside the loop, we multiply `product` by the current number `i`.
- After the loop completes, we print the value of `product`.
