```rust
fn main() {
    let sum = (1..=20).filter(|&x| x % 2 == 0).sum();
    print(sum);
}
```

Explanation:
- The `range(1..=20)` generates a range from 1 to 20 inclusive.
- The `filter(|&x| x % 2 == 0)` filters the range to include only even numbers.
- The `sum()` function calculates the sum of the filtered even numbers.
