```rust
fn main() {
    let mut product = 1;
    for num in 1..=6 {
        product *= num;
    }
    println!("{}", product);
}
```

This program initializes a mutable variable `product` to 1. It then iterates over the numbers from 1 to 6 (inclusive) using a range, multiplying each number by `product`. After the loop completes, it prints out the final product of all these numbers.
