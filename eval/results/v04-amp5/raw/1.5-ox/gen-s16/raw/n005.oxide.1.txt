```rust
fn main() {
    let sum = (1..=20).filter(|&x| x % 2 == 0).sum::<Int>();
    println!("{}", sum);
}
```

This Rust program calculates and prints the total of all even numbers between 1 and 20 inclusive. The `filter` function is used to select only the even numbers, and the `sum` method computes their total.
