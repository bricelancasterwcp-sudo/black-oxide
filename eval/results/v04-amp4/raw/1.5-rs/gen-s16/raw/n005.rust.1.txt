```rust
fn main() {
    let sum = (1..=20).filter(|&x| x % 2 == 0).sum::<u32>();
    println!("The total of all even numbers between 1 and 20 inclusive is: {}", sum);
}
```

Explanation:
- `(1..=20)` creates an iterator from 1 to 20.
- `filter(|&x| x % 2 == 0)` filters out the odd numbers, keeping only the even ones.
- `.sum::<u32>()` calculates the sum of all even numbers in the iterator.
- `println!()` prints the result.
