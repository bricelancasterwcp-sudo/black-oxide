    }

}

- We start by initializing `a` and `b` to 1, as per the sequence's definition.
- We then enter a loop that runs from 2 to 9 (inclusive), incrementing `i` by 1 in each iteration.
- Inside the loop, we calculate the sum of the two previous terms (`temp = a + b`) and assign it to `b`.
- After updating `a` and `b`, we reassign them with their new values. This effectively moves `a` from its old position to be used for the next iteration.
- Finally, after the loop finishes, we print the value of `b`, which represents the tenth term in the sequence.
