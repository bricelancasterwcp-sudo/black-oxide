```rust
fn main() {
    let sum = (1..=20).filter(|&x| x % 2 == 0).sum();
    print(sum);
}
```

**Explanation**:
- We use `range(1, 21)` to create an iterator from 1 to 20.
- The `filter` method is used with a closure to keep only the even numbers.
- The `sum` method then calculates the sum of these even numbers.
