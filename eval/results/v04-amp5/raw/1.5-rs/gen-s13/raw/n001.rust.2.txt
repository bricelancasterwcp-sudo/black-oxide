```rust
fn main() {
    let sum = (1..=5).fold(0, |acc, x| acc + i32::pow(x, 3));
    println!("The sum of cubes from 1 to 5 is: {}", sum);
}
```

Explanation:
In the original program, `x.pow(3)` was ambiguous because Rust cannot determine the type of `x` in this context. By adding `i32::pow`, we explicitly specify that we want to perform the power operation on integers.
