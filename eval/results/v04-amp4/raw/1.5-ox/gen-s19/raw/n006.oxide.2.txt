fn main() {
    let mut biggest = 0;
    for i in 0..=9 {
        if 2i.pow(i) > 100 {

        biggest = i;
    }
    print(biggest);

The problem was due to the use of semicolons and incorrect indentation in the loop. The `break` statement needs to be inside a loop, and the loop itself should have its own block to ensure it is closed properly.
