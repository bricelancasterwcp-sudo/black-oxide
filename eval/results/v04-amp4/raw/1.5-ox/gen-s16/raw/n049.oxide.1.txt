```rust
fn main() {
    let figs = vec![8, 3, 11, 6];
    println!("Smallest figure: {}", min(figs));
    println!("Gap between largest and smallest: {}", max(figs) - min(figs));
}
```
In this program, the `min` and `max` functions are used to find the smallest and largest elements in the vector `figs`, respectively. The difference between these two values is then printed out.
