```rust
fn main() {
    let mut result = 1;
    for i in 1..=6 {
        result *= i;
    }
    println!("The product of numbers from 1 to 6 is: {}", result);
}
```
This program defines a mutable variable `result` initialized to 1, and then iterates over the range 1 to 6 (inclusive) using a for loop. During each iteration, it multiplies the current value of `i` with `result`. After the loop completes, it prints out the final product of all numbers from 1 to 6.
